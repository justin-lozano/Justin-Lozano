# Data

## Electricity research paper

The research uses the following sources:

- April–July 2026 electricity statements, covering service from February 21 through June 22, 2026, for the bill decomposition.
- Historical electricity-use and billing records for context.
- HECO’s published adjustments and applicable rate schedule for the external billing check.
- A potential vendor’s LED estimate for evaluating project cost and electricity savings.

Original facility statements and the vendor estimate are retained outside this repository under its confidentiality rules.

## Verification status

A preliminary bill decomposition was developed with AI assistance. Transcribed values, formulas, and charge classifications remain subject to verification against the original statements. The external comparison must account for actual service dates.

The full bill decomposition and supporting vendor figures will be provided directly to Adam through a private submission, outside GitHub. This repository will document the sources, calculation method, and verification status without publishing the underlying facility figures.
